# Breadboard

[Breadboard] is chosen as the base of these assemblies to eliminate the need of soldering.

The internal structure of the breadboard shown below reveals that 5 holes in the middle of the board are all connected and holes along each vertical lines on the sides of the board are also connected:

![Breadboard Internal Structure](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e8/Breadboard.png)

Integrated Circuit (IC) chips in [DIP] package should be laid across the middle groove:

![IC chip layout on a breadboard](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXc2AXjHyGxGIC1fNTvuwa_XcRwWIvz5KhgeUJukZutaKI8cUQCAZktRb2FDQFHdwAjnvUepC6DPKSRuQLdZ42CwhTJe_SH90u47x7HOmzQXaBKOGqnS1e_O2eSOMaQJKc8srF88Lg?key=XDpCo9hSgO1Ycb19zQB5DQ)
